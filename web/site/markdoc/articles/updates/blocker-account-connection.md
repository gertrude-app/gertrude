{% .lead %} **TL;DR**&mdash;Today we're rolling out **account connection** — a way to link
your child's iPhone or iPad to a free Gertrude parent account. You get all the same
blocking, but now you can control it remotely from the Gertrude parent website.

## How it works

The [Gertrude iOS app](/iphone-and-ipad) closes Screen Time loopholes and blocks unwanted
content like animated GIF search, Spotify images, Apple Maps, and more. But until now, the
only time you could decide **what** Gertrude blocked was during the initial installation —
and that was by design, because it was the only moment we knew for sure the parent was the
one holding the device.

Starting now, you can choose to connect the iPhone or iPad to a free Gertrude account
during the setup process by entering a 6-digit code from the parent website. Once
connected, the device will show up associated with your child.

{% image src="ios-account-connection/connected-ios-device.png" alt="Gertrude parent dashboard showing a child's connected iPhone alongside a Mac" caption="A connected iPhone shows up right in your parent dashboard" /%}

## What does it give you?

The main thing is the ability to **change blocking rules at any time** — from any computer
or phone — without having to pick up your kid's device. Also, when we release new block
groups, you'll be able to opt into them from your dashboard too. Without a connection, the
device is locked to whatever rules you chose when it was installed.

{% image src="ios-account-connection/connected-ios-device-controls.png" alt="Block group controls for a connected iPhone showing toggles for ads, AI features, GIFs, Spotify, and more" caption="Toggle block groups on or off remotely from your dashboard" /%}

This is also the foundation for new controls and features as we build them out. The core
blocking works great on its own, but connecting to an account is how you'll get access to
everything we add down the road.

## How much does it cost?

Connecting to a Gertrude account is free, and you get all the same blocking as if you were
using the app (which has always been free) without an account.

We may add more premium controls and features in the future, but the core blocking
features will always be free.

That said, if the iPhone or iPad is used by an individual who is _over 18 years of age,_
then you either need to
[supervise the device yourself with Apple Configurator](https://youtu.be/G7W1d0EWmmQ), or
supervise it with a $10/year paid Gertrude light account in order to use Gertrude on that
device. The supervision requirement is imposed on us by Apple — it's not our decision.

## How do I set it up?

It only takes a minute:

1. Go to [https://parents.gertrude.app](https://parents.gertrude.app) and create a free
   account (or log in if you already have one)
2. Add a child and choose to connect an iPhone or iPad
3. You'll get a 6-digit code — type it into the Gertrude app on the phone

That's it. The device is now linked to your account.
