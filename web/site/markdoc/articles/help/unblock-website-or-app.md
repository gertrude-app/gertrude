Gertrude blocks internet access by default. Websites are allowed through **keys** grouped
into **keychains**. Installed Mac apps can either use a keychain or be granted
unrestricted internet access.

## Unblock a website with a public keychain

Public keychains are prebuilt for popular websites and apps. Try one before creating your
own keys.

1. Sign in to the [Gertrude parent website](https://parents.gertrude.app).
2. Select **Children**, choose your child, and open their Mac settings.
3. Select **Manage filtering**, then **Add keychain**.
4. Select **Public keychains** and search for the website or service.
5. Select the keychain, click **Add keychain**, and then click **Save settings**.

Changes are sent to the child’s Mac automatically. Reload the website after the settings
have been saved.

## Unblock a website with your own keychain

If there isn’t a public keychain for the website:

1. Select **Keychains** in the parent website.
2. Click **Create keychain**, enter a name, and click **Create keychain** again.
3. Open the new keychain and click **Create key**.
4. Enter the website address. For most websites, you can leave the other options
   unchanged.
5. Click **Create key**.
6. Return to the child’s **Manage filtering** screen, click **Add keychain**, and select
   your new keychain.
7. Click **Save settings**, then reload the website on the child’s Mac.

A website may load only partially after you create its first key. If that happens, see
[why a website can still look broken](/help/mac/website-still-broken-after-unblocking).

## Grant an app unrestricted internet access

For an installed app that should be allowed to make any internet request:

1. Select **Children** in the parent website, choose your child, and open their Mac
   settings.
2. Find the **Apps** section and click its button. It says **Pick apps** when the child
   has no blocked apps yet, and **Manage apps** otherwise.
3. Under **Unrestricted internet apps**, click **Pick apps to grant internet** or **Grant
   internet to apps**.
4. Select the app and click **Grant internet**.
5. Click **Save settings**.

Grant unrestricted internet only to an app you trust. If you want to allow only specific
addresses for the app, use keys instead.
