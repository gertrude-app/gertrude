Gertrude for Mac has a built-in health check that looks for common problems and, when
possible, gives you a button to fix them.

## Step 1: Open the Gertrude menu

Click the **Gertrude icon** in the menu bar at the top of the Mac screen. Then click the
**gear icon** in the lower-right corner of the menu.

{% image src="administrate.png" caption="Open the Gertrude menu and click the gear icon" alt="The Gertrude menu with an arrow pointing to the gear icon" /%}

Approve the Mac’s administrator authentication when prompted.

## Step 2: Review the health check

The health check starts automatically. A healthy setup shows green check marks. Depending
on how your family uses Gertrude, it may check:

- The installed Gertrude app and filter versions
- Filter status, communication, and loaded rules
- Screen recording, keystroke recording, and Full Disk Access permissions
- Notification settings
- The current macOS user account type
- Your Gertrude account status
- Conflicts with Apple’s Screen Time web filter

{% image src="health-check.png" caption="A healthy Gertrude installation shows green check marks" alt="The Gertrude health check showing that every check passed" /%}

## Step 3: Fix any reported problems

If a check reports a warning or failure, read its explanation and use the action button
beside it. The available button depends on the problem—for example, Gertrude may offer to
fix a permission, enable or repair the filter, refresh its rules, or open the relevant
System Settings screen.

## Step 4: Run the check again

After making a change, click **Recheck**. If Gertrude tells you to restart the Mac,
restart it before checking again.
