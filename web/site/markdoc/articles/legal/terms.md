## Who Gertrude Is For

Gertrude is for people protecting themselves and the people close to them. That includes:

- **Parents and guardians** filtering, supervising, or monitoring devices used by children
  in their household.
- **Adults protecting themselves.** Putting Gertrude on your own devices — for
  self-control, for recovery, or simply to build a healthier relationship with the
  internet — is a first-class use of this product, not a workaround. A lot of our
  customers use Gertrude exactly this way.
- **Accountability partners.** Two adults who have agreed to it, where one holds the keys
  and sees the activity for the other.
- **Spouses and partners** who have decided together to put Gertrude on one or both of
  their devices.
- **Schools and similar communities** protecting devices they provide to their students,
  where the students and their families know Gertrude is there. This isn't our main use
  case, but we're glad to support it — [contact us](/contact) first so we can make sure
  your account is set up sensibly.

What all of those have in common is that the people Gertrude protects know it's there, and
it's there out of care for them. That's the line. Gertrude is not a tool for controlling
someone who hasn't agreed to it, and it's not a tool for managing devices as business
assets.

You must be at least 18 years old to create a Gertrude account, and you're responsible for
everything done under it.

## Acceptable Use

**You may use Gertrude to:**

- Filter, supervise, or monitor devices used by children in your household, as their
  parent or guardian.
- Protect your own devices from content you've decided you don't want access to.
- Hold accountability for another adult — or let another adult hold it for you — where
  both of you know about it and have freely agreed to it.

**You may not use Gertrude to:**

- Install, run, or configure any Gertrude app on someone's device without their knowledge.
  **Gertrude is never to be used secretly.** Everyone protected by Gertrude has a right to
  know it's there.
- Restrict or monitor an adult's device without their free and informed consent, including
  covert monitoring of a spouse, partner, or other adult. Consent given under pressure
  isn't consent.
- Enforce a loan, lease, rent-to-own, installment plan, or any other financing arrangement
  — including locking, restricting, or disabling a device because a payment was missed or
  late.
- Hold a device as collateral, or repossess, brick, or remotely disable a device on behalf
  of a lender, retailer, creditor, or collections operation.
- Manage devices belonging to employees, contractors, or other workers, or to run a
  commercial device fleet.
- Resell, rebrand, or bundle Gertrude as part of a product or service you provide to
  others.
- Create multiple accounts, or otherwise work around a limit we've placed on your account.
- Break the law, or help anyone else break it.

{% callout type="warning" title="Gertrude is never secret" %}

Gertrude is designed to be visible, and we intend it to stay that way. If you're deciding
on someone else's behalf — a parent for a child, a school for its students — they still
need to know Gertrude is protecting their device. You decide for them; you don't hide it
from them. Between adults, both people must know and agree. Secret installation is the one
thing we will terminate an account over without a conversation first.

{% /callout %}

**Gertrude is not an MDM.** It is not a mobile device management platform, an
asset-tracking system, or a repossession tool, and we don't support using it as one. If
you need to manage devices for a business, please use a product built for that.

If you're doing something unusual but legitimate — a ministry running accountability
relationships between consenting adults, say, a recovery group, or a large family —
[contact us](/contact). We're glad to make room for real cases.
