{% .lead .mb-12 %} Today we're excited to announce the 1.0 release of
**[Gertrude Music](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gertrude-music/id6782194077)**, a
parent-controlled music app for iPhone and iPad. A parent or accountability partner
chooses which artists and albums are available. The listener gets a beautiful,
full-featured music app&mdash;but no discovery feeds, recommendations, or access to music
that wasn't approved.

{% image src="gertrude-music-v1.png" noBorder=true alt="Gertrude Music app icon over a library of album artwork" /%}

## The problem: most music apps are built for discovery

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and other music apps are designed to help you find
more. For most listeners, that's part of what makes them great: search an enormous
catalog, follow recommendations, and move endlessly from one artist or album to the next.

But that design creates a problem when you want your child to enjoy music independently
without putting an open music catalog at their fingertips and giving them access to the
immense amount of explicit or otherwise inappropriate music available.

Even when a service offers an explicit-content filter, it doesn't let you choose exactly
which music your child can access. And an explicit label answers only one narrow question.
A song can be free of profanity and still contain themes your family isn't comfortable
with. Meanwhile, search results, recommendations, autoplay, artist pages, and album
artwork keep presenting music you haven't reviewed.

## A simple solution: a parent or accountability partner approves the library

Gertrude Music is built on the same philosophy as the rest of our products: the only way
to create something truly safe is to start with nothing and only add only what is known to
be safe, rather than starting with everything and trying to block the bad parts. We built
a platform that gives parents complete control over what music is available in the app,
and then let parents decide what music is safe for their children rather than trying to
decide that ourselves like other apps do.

From the Gertrude parents website, you search for music and choose what to make available.
Those choices become the listener's entire music library. They can browse and search
inside it, but there is no way to search an open music catalog, wander through a discovery
feed, or follow recommendations into music you didn't select. You can also remove
previously allowed music whenever you need to.

The result is a simple division of responsibility: **you choose what is available; they
choose what to play.**

Although we usually describe this as a parent and child, the same approach works for teens
or adults who want a trusted accountability partner to hold the boundary around their
music choices.

## Nothing else. Nothing missing.

We didn't want safety to make listening feel like a punishment. Gertrude Music gives the
listener a polished, full-featured app for enjoying the library you've chosen. They can
create playlists from their approved music library, edit and reorder the queue, search
within their approved library, and so much more, all within a beautiful native app
designed to delight.

Playlists give the listener room to make the library their own without becoming a backdoor
to new music. A playlist never grants permission to play a song; everything in it must
still come from the approved library.

## Try Gertrude Music today

Gertrude Music is available now in the
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gertrude-music/id6782194077). Download it on the
listener's iPhone or iPad, then follow the setup steps to connect the device to your
Gertrude account. Once it's connected, use the parents website to choose the artists or
albums you want to make available.

To use Gertrude Music, you need:

1. An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or newer
2. An active Apple Music subscription on the listener's device for music licensing and
   playback
3. A Gertrude Medium or Full subscription

### Pricing: $5/month for your whole family, with no ads ever

Gertrude Medium costs **$5/month** and covers Gertrude Music for every listener in your
family. If you already subscribe to Gertrude Full, Music is included in your existing
plan. The app itself is free to download, and Gertrude never shows ads.

You choose the music. They get a delightful listening app that shows only what you've
approved&mdash;no discovery feeds or recommendations.
