If you're an existing Gertrude account owner, you can now share a personalized referral
link with your friends via email, text, social media, or however you like. For everyone
who clicks your link and becomes a paying Gertrude customer, you get $30 in account
credit. There's no limit to how much credit you can earn, so share away!

{% referral-share-link /%}

## How it works

- You share your personalized referral link with a friend.
- Your friend starts a _Full_ subscription free trial to use the Mac app.
- They get the normal **21-day free trial**, then their **first paid month is free.**
- After your friend's **first successful payment**, you receive a **$30 Gertrude account
  credit.**

{% callout title="Good to Know" %}

- We'll notify you by email when someone signs up from your referral link, and again when
  you earn a credit so you can keep track of your referrals.
- The $30 credit is earned after their first successful $10 payment, after the free trial
  and free month expire, so expect a little delay.
- Credits apply to future Gertrude invoices. They are not cash and are not transferable.
- There's no limit to how many credits you can earn, so share with as many friends as
  you'd like!
- The program is initially for existing Gertrude Mac app customers invited into the pilot.
  If you don't have a referral code and want one, [let us know](/contact).
