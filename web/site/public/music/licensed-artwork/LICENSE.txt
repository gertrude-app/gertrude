GERTRUDE HOMEPAGE ALBUM ARTWORK

Source and license review: 2026-09-05

These are third-party copyrighted album covers, not Gertrude-owned artwork.
The licenses below apply to the respective image files and our adaptations of
that artwork. They do not license Gertrude's branding or application code.
Artist and label endorsement is not implied. Selection in the example library
is illustrative, not a judgment about the other albums shown.

Modifications by Gertrude: made square, up to 640 x 640 pixels, and converted
to WebP. Lower-resolution sources have not been enlarged.
Non-square originals have been padded without cropping or stretching the art.
The homepage applies rounded masks, animated movement and scaling, shadows,
opacity, brightness, and saturation effects. Our artwork adaptations are
available under the same license listed for each original below.

Each source page reports that Wikimedia's Volunteer Response Team reviewed
copyright-holder permission. Ticket references are recorded below; the private
permission correspondence itself is not publicly accessible.
