The already-cropped PNG files are converted to WebP at their original
resolution, with quality 94 and alpha quality 100. The page displays the
complete artwork with small static rotations, without added device shadows
or altered screens. A clockwise entrance fades, sharpens, and moves the
compositions outward after the heading highlight. Reduced motion shows the
final arrangement immediately. These images are not covered by any license
for Gertrude's own artwork.
