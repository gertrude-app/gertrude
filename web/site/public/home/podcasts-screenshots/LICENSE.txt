GERTRUDE PODCASTS HOMEPAGE SCREENSHOTS

Captured by Kiah in Dark Mode from a local Gertrude Podcasts development build
on an iPhone 15. The original captures are 1179 x 2556 pixels. App content,
status-bar time, battery status, and controls have not been retouched.

Source captures and distributed compositions:
IMG_0611.PNG -> shows-810.webp, shows-1350.webp
IMG_0612.PNG -> show-details-810.webp, show-details-1350.webp
IMG_0613.PNG -> pin-810.webp, pin-1350.webp

Each capture is proportionally fitted to the device's display opening, with a
small edge crop to match its aspect ratio, and masked beneath the Apple bezel.
The complete composition is resized and converted to WebP. The page applies a
staggered fade and vertical entrance to each composition.

BACKGROUND ARTWORK

The page reuses /home/music-screenshots/background.webp, converted from
image 10.png supplied by Kiah on 2026-10-01. The texture is masked from
invisible at the top to 5% opacity at the bottom, over a stone-colored
background that transitions smoothly between dark and light.

PODCAST AND AUDIOBOOK COVER ARTWORK

The library capture displays the four LibriVox covers below and New Rustacean.
The show-details capture also displays New Rustacean. Were You Raised By Wolves?
is shown with Gertrude's built-in generic radio illustration, not that
publisher's cover art. The PIN capture contains no podcast artwork.

The shows illustrate a family-chosen library, not endorsements by their
publishers or recommendations that every episode is suitable for every child.
No audio is redistributed. Names identify their respective shows and books;
they are not Gertrude branding.
