GERTRUDE MUSIC HOMEPAGE SCREENSHOTS

Captured by Kiah on 2026-11-09 from a Gertrude Music development build running
in iOS Simulator. Playback status, time, and controls have not been retouched.

Source captures and distributed compositions:
Screenshot Music iPad Pro 13 11-09-2026 at 3.25.54 PM.png -> ipad-1200.webp, ipad-2000.webp
Screenshot iPhone Duo 11-09-2026 at 3.26.16 PM.png -> duo-480.webp, duo-900.webp
Screenshot iPhone 18 Pro 11-09-2026 at 3.26.52 PM.png -> iphone-420.webp, iphone-810.webp

Each capture exactly matches its device frame's screen opening and is placed
inside it without scaling or cropping. The complete device composition is
resized and converted to WebP. Device images are not tilted, cropped, or
altered; the homepage adds a soft drop shadow beneath the two phones and
overlaps them in front of the iPad. The compositions enter with a staggered
fade and translation.

BACKGROUND ARTWORK

background.webp comes from image 10.png, supplied by Kiah on 2026-10-01.
The 1800 x 1230 original is converted to WebP without resizing. The page masks
the texture from invisible at the top to 5% opacity at the bottom, over a
