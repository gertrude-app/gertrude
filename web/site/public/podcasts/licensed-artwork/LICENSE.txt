GERTRUDE HOMEPAGE PODCAST ARTWORK
Sources and published reuse permissions checked 2026-09-10.

These are illustrations of a family-chosen library, not endorsements by the
publishers or recommendations that every episode is suitable for every child.
No audio is redistributed. Two entries are public-domain audiobook podcast feeds.
Some shows are completed archives. Names and marks identify their respective
shows; they are not Gertrude branding.

The downloaded TuxDigital WebP files are reproduced byte-for-byte, without
cropping, masking, retouching, or recoloring. Their CC BY-ND license permits
commercial redistribution but not sharing adaptations. Preserve these originals.
The TuxDigital covers sit inside rounded, color-matched frames with a 4 px inset;
the artwork itself is not clipped. The other six files were proportionally
resized (no enlargement, maximum 640 px) and converted to WebP; their presentation
uses rounded corner masks. The demo scales and moves images and changes the
opacity of its interface.

Permissions below cover the artwork, not Gertrude's application code or branding.
Wikimedia entries here are uploader-published grants, not VRT-reviewed permissions.
