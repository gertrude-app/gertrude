Gertrude 2.9 Released!

Today we're announcing version 2.9 — our biggest Mac app update ever. It includes a
completely rebuilt setup wizard, always-blocked items, an unfiltered mode, major browser
and performance improvements, privacy enhancements, and much more.

View this email on our blog: https://gertrude.app/updates/gertrude-for-mac-2-9-0


A Completely Rebuilt Setup Experience

For years, the hardest part of Gertrude was getting it installed. We always told
parents: "Setup is a bit tricky, but once you're running you'll love it." Not anymore.
The new setup wizard walks parents step-by-step through every decision — blocking apps,
granting other apps internet access, configuring downtime, choosing always-blocked
items, picking public keychains, setting up text notifications, and more. If you had
trouble getting Gertrude set up before, this is the version to try again.


Always-Blocked Items

You can now mark certain things as always blocked, even during filter suspensions. Opt
into any of four curated groups — adult content, Messages GIF search, social media, and
Spotlight searches — or add your own custom rules.


Unfiltered Internet Mode

Most Gertrude users want the internet filtered. But a small, important subset wants all
the monitoring, oversight, and accountability Gertrude provides without blocking the
internet — often for older teens, or adults in accountability relationships. Unfiltered
mode is now a first-class option. Screenshots, keystroke logging, security events,
blocked apps, and always-blocked items all still work, giving you real protection
without a restrictive filter.


Major Improvements for Chrome and Firefox

Gertrude has always been just as safe for non-Safari browsers, but it's been noticeably
easier to use in Safari: fewer keys to unlock, clearer blocked-request messages, better
public-keychain support. With v2.9, Gertrude pulls comparable data from raw socket
flows, making Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers much nicer to use.


10-50x Faster Filter Decisions

Every network request on your child's computer gets checked against every key attached
to their Gertrude user. We've always been fast — microseconds at worst — but v2.9
includes a major performance rewrite. In our benchmarks the new filter runs 10 to 50
times faster. For children with many keys, this is noticeable.


Private-by-Default Screenshots

Our Mac app screenshots have always been private, but if a screenshot URL were ever
accidentally shared, the image could be visible to non-parents. (We have zero records of
this ever happening.) v2.9 switches to private-by-default images with short-lived signed
URLs, eliminating this class of vulnerability entirely.


Batch Unlock Requests

Responding to a flood of unlock requests used to require a lot of clicks. The parent's
website (https://parents.gertrude.app) has been overhauled so you can accept or deny
whole groups of unlock requests at once — a big quality-of-life improvement, especially
early on when kids are still getting comfortable with the keychain setup.


Ntfy — A New Notification Method

In addition to email, text, and Slack, Gertrude now supports ntfy (https://ntfy.sh/), a
free push notification service with iOS and Android apps. If you're currently receiving
text notifications, switching to ntfy saves us a real chunk of money — and delivery is
just as fast.


What Next?
