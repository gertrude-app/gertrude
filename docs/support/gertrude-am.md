# Gertrude AM

Gertrude AM is a separate iOS podcast app for kids. It is simpler than the Mac app and the
main iOS blocker app, and it does **not** connect to the Gertrude parent dashboard or to a
Gertrude account.

## What It Is

- A basic podcast app with one core parental-control idea: adding new podcasts is gated by
  a PIN.
- Built for parents who want a child to be able to listen to approved podcasts without
  being able to freely search for and add anything they want.
- Initial release was **October 23, 2025**.

## How It Works

- During onboarding, the parent/protector can set a 6-digit PIN and confirm it.
- After that, the child can listen to already-added shows normally.
- To search for and add a new podcast feed, the app requires the PIN.
- The PIN can also be changed later from the app's settings area by confirming the current
  PIN.

## What It Does Not Do

- It does not use Screen Time authorization.
- It does not install a network filter.
- It does not connect to the Gertrude dashboard.
- It does not connect to a Gertrude parent account.
- It is not managed like the Mac app or the main iOS app.

## Subscription

- Gertrude AM uses Apple's built-in in-app purchase system.
- Costs **$10/year after a 30-day trial**, but family-sharing means one subscription works
  for multiple devices in the same Apple family.
- This subscription is handled inside the app through Apple, not through the Gertrude
  dashboard/billing system.

## Support Notes

- Support volume is relatively low compared with the Mac app and main iOS app.
- The most likely support topics are:
  - setting or changing the PIN
  - managing the Apple in-app subscription
  - basic podcast-app behavior like adding shows or reclaiming storage
