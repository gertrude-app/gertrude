# Product Overview

Gertrude has three customer-facing apps and one supporting parent dashboard. The main
products are the Mac app, the iOS app, and Gertrude AM, an iOS podcast app. A fourth iOS
app, Gertrude Music, exists in the codebase (`swift/music`) but is in private beta (App
Store review) and not yet released — don't treat it as a shipped product. The parent
dashboard at `https://parents.gertrude.app` is important, but it is better understood as the
management surface for the main apps rather than a separate standalone product.

## Main Products

- **Mac app:** the most full-featured Gertrude product. It runs on a child's Mac and
  combines internet filtering with monitoring and parent-review features.
- **iOS app:** a more limited iPhone/iPad product. It helps parents apply internet
  restrictions on iOS, but it does not offer the same depth of control or monitoring as
  the Mac app.
- **Gertrude AM:** a separate iOS podcast app with lightweight parental controls around
  podcast listening.
- **Parent dashboard:** the web app parents use to manage children, connect devices,
  review activity, adjust settings, and respond to requests generated by the Mac app and,
  in newer versions, parts of the iOS app experience.

## How They Relate

- The **Mac app** is tightly connected to the parent dashboard and depends on it for most
  parent-controlled configuration.
- The **iOS app** is a separate product with a more constrained feature set because of iOS
  platform limits; its relationship to the dashboard is newer and more limited than the
  Mac app's.
- **Gertrude AM** is its own product line and does not connect to the parent dashboard.
- The **dashboard** is the parent's control center across the family account, but the real
  child-facing products are the apps themselves.
