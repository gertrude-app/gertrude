# Product Overview

Gertrude has four customer-facing apps and one supporting parent dashboard. The products
are the Mac app, the iOS app, Gertrude Podcasts, and Gertrude Music. Gertrude Music is a
publicly available iOS app whose code lives in `swift/music`. Like the other apps, it
connects to a Gertrude account. The parent dashboard at `https://parents.gertrude.app` is
important, but it is better understood as the management surface for the main apps rather
than a separate standalone product.

## Main Products

- **Mac app:** the most full-featured Gertrude product. It runs on a child's Mac and
  combines internet filtering with monitoring and parent-review features.
- **iOS app:** a more limited iPhone/iPad product. It helps parents apply internet
  restrictions on iOS, but it does not offer the same depth of control or monitoring as
  the Mac app.
- **Gertrude Podcasts:** a separate iOS podcast app with lightweight parental controls around
  podcast listening (adding new podcasts is gated by a PIN). Connects to a Gertrude
  account, requires Light subscription after 30-day free trial.
- **Gertrude Music:** an iOS app for listening to a parent-curated Apple Music library. A
  child can organize that approved music into playlists that sync through Gertrude;
  playlist membership never grants permission to play music the parent has not approved.
  The app connects to a Gertrude account.
- **Parent dashboard:** the web app parents use to manage children, connect devices,
  review activity, adjust settings, and respond to requests generated by the Mac app and,
  in newer versions, parts of the iOS app experience.

## How They Relate

- The **Mac app** is tightly connected to the parent dashboard and depends on it for most
  parent-controlled configuration.
- The **iOS app** is a separate product with a more constrained feature set because of iOS
  platform limits; its relationship to the dashboard is newer and more limited than the
  Mac app's, though it is growing.
- **Gertrude Podcasts** connects to a Gertrude account for subscription management and PIN
  reset.
- **Gertrude Music** likewise connects to a Gertrude account.
- The **Dashboard** (https://parents.gertrude.app) is the parent's control center across
  the family account, but the real child-facing products are the apps themselves.
